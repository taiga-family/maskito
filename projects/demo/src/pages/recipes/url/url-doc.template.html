Use a permissive mask to guide URL input while leaving final URL validation to your form or application.

By default, the mask accepts absolute URLs without whitespace, including http:// , https:// , bare domains and subdomains. Set httpsOnly to require an https:// prefix, or allowRelative to accept relative URLs such as /test/test . The recipe intentionally does not validate whether a domain or TLD exists. Keep final URL and IANA TLD validation outside of the mask.
