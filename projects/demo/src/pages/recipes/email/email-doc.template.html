Use a permissive mask to guide email input while leaving final email validation to your form or application.

Don't use <input type="email" /> with Maskito! See the only supported input types . inputmode="email" and autocomplete="email" remain available.

The mask accepts intermediate states such as user@ , rejects whitespace and multiple @ symbols, and uses a postprocessor to remove the mailto: prefix. It intentionally does not validate a complete email address.
